04 / BASIC VISUALS

Choose the chart that answers the question.

A chart helps someone compare values, follow a change over time or spot unusual values. Choose the chart and calculation that fit the question.

REPORT VIEW CHART CHOICE NUMBER CHOICE

Once the data has been checked and cleaned, choose a chart that makes the comparison easy to read. Say which rows are grouped, which number is calculated and what happens when someone clicks the chart.

Match the question to the chart

↔

Compare groups

Use a bar or column chart when the question is “which stores or departments differ?”

↗

Follow a change

Use a line chart when the order of dates matters.

▤

See the spread

Use a table, histogram or summary when you need to see the range and unusual values.

Build the first two charts

1

Markdown1 by store

In Report view choose Clustered Bar Chart. Drag Store to the category axis and Markdown1 to Values.

You should see: one bar for each store, normally using Sum.
Why: Store creates the groups; Markdown1 supplies the number.
2

Try a different calculation

Open the Markdown1 field menu and try Count (Distinct) as well as Sum.

You should see: the bars change because the calculation has changed.
Why: Sum and Count (Distinct) answer different questions.
3

Temperature over time

Choose Line Chart, drag Date to the axis and Temperature to Values. Change Temperature from Sum to Median.

You should see: a time-series line using Power BI's date hierarchy.
Why: adding temperatures together does not give a useful typical temperature.
4

Change the date detail

Try Go to the next level, Expand all down one level, and Drill up.

You should see: the visual move between year, quarter, month and day detail.
Why: the date hierarchy lets you move from years to days without rebuilding the chart.

How Power BI combines values

Field Calculation Question it answers Check
Weekly_Sales Sum What is the total over this group and period? Do the dates and row meaning fit the total?
Temperature Median What is a typical temperature in this group? Sum would add unlike measurements together.
Markdown1 Sum or distinct count What total amount or how many distinct values occurred? Count and Sum describe different things.

What happens when you select a chart?

F

Filter

The other chart shows only the related rows.

H

Highlight

The selected part stands out while the rest stays visible.

—

None

The other chart does not change.

Check your chart before moving to the model
  1. What question does the visual answer?
  2. What groups or dates are shown?
  3. How is the number being calculated?
  4. What happens when you select one bar?

Check: you should be able to name the group, number, dates and click behaviour.
